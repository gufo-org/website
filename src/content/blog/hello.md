Gufo is small on purpose: one machine, a short list of models, and measurements for every change. The docs say *what* Gufo does. These notes are for *why*: the experiments that failed, the numbers that changed our minds and the designs that are still being built.

## What to expect

- **Engineering:** deep dives into one mechanism at a time, often with live figures you can step through.
- **Measured:** benchmark write-ups with their full settings, so you can reproduce them.
- **Notes:** short posts like this one.

Every post links to the design document, card or measurement it's based on. If a number isn't measured yet, it says so.

> This post is plain Markdown. Posts that need interactive figures are written in MDX instead; both live in `src/content/blog/`.
